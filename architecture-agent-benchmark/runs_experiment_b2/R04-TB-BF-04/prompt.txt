## Ticket: Show the area of a selected shape, not just its width and height

People using the app for floor plans, diagrams, and technical sketches keep asking for a
way to see the area a shape covers, not just its bounding box width and height. Right now
the side panel that shows live measurements for whatever is selected (position, size,
angle, etc.) only shows width and height, which isn't enough — a rotated rectangle, a
diamond, an ellipse, and a hand-drawn or multi-point closed outline all cover different
areas even when their bounding boxes look similar, and users have to do the math
themselves.

Please add a live area readout to that panel for the selected shape. It should:

- Update immediately as the shape is moved, resized, or reshaped.
- Be correct for all shape types that have a meaningful enclosed area — rectangles,
  diamonds, ellipses, and any closed/filled outline shapes (including hand-drawn ones),
  not just axis-aligned rectangles. A rotated shape must report the same area as before
  rotation, not the area of its (larger) bounding box.
- Not show a misleading number for shapes that don't enclose a region (e.g. an open line
  or a text label) — it's fine to simply omit the readout for those.
- Work the same way whether one shape is selected or the panel is otherwise showing
  measurements for a single selected shape.

Use sensible units/rounding consistent with how width and height are already displayed
in that panel.
